The model copies the word that followed the current word earlier in the text, in two stages that work the same way. Previous-token heads (L1.H3 and L2.H12 early, L15.H3 and L20.H0 late) look one position back and write, at every position, which word came just before it: the variable prev. Induction heads (L3.H10 early, L16.H14 and L21.H8 late) compare the current word with prev, attend to the position right after the earlier copy of the current word, and copy the word found there into the prediction; L21.H8 matters most. Swapping prev from another prompt makes the induction heads copy whatever followed the current word in that prompt.
