The model copies the word that followed the current word earlier in the text, in two stages that work the same way. Previous-token heads (L1.H3 and L2.H12 early, L15.H3 and L20.H0 late) write, at every position, which word came just before it. An induction head's key reads that write, so its query for the current word A finds the position right after the earlier A; its value there is the word that followed A, which it copies into the output. L3.H10 does this early; L16.H14 and L21.H8 do it late, and L21.H8 matters most. Everything else, including the early heads and MLPs that every prompt needs, is the same on the edited prompt and is left as it is there.
